The lemon groves smelled divine today. We found a hidden staircase leading down from the main piazza that bypassed all the tourist crowds. Every step revealed a new angle of the pastel houses clinging to the cliffside like architectural barnacles.

We stopped for a quick espresso at a place called 'Bar Internazionale'. The owner told us about his grandfather who used to fish these waters long before the hotels were built. It's moments like these that make the trip feel real—not just a sequence of sights, but a collection of stories.
